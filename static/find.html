How this works

  1. Keep this page open on a laptop/Mac on the same Wi‑Fi as the TV
  2. Open the Camera app on your phone and point at the QR
  3. The free remote opens with your TV already connected

Phone Safari cannot safely scan every device on Wi‑Fi by itself — a computer on the network does the finding for you.
