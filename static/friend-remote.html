Free Roku remote on your phone. No App Store. No microphone needed.

  • 1Phone on the same Wi‑Fi as your TV
  • 2TV: Settings → System → Advanced → Control by mobile apps → Enabled
  • 3Allow Local Network, then scan for your TV

1. Allow Local Network

Your phone must allow this site to look for TVs on Wi‑Fi. Tap the button — if a popup appears, choose Allow.

If no popup appears (iPhone):
  1. Open the iPhone Settings app
  2. Scroll to Privacy & Security → Local Network
  3. Turn on the switch for Safari (or TV Remote if you added it to Home Screen)
  4. Come back here and tap the button again, then Scan for TVs

Android / Chrome: when the banner asks for local network / nearby devices, tap Allow.

2. Find your TV

Tap “Allow Local Network” above first, then scan.

Or type the last number of the TV IP (Settings → Network → About):
