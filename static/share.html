Install the free remote

Main product: remote on your phone (Home Screen). No App Store. No microphone. Voice is optional for advanced users only.

1. Open on your phone

https://kurbaitaev.github.io/hisense-remote/

2. Install on Home Screen

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the link
  2. Tap Share
  3. Add to Home Screen → Add

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link
  2. Menu ⋮
  3. Add to Home screen / Install app

Also enable on the TV: Settings → System → Advanced → Control by mobile apps → Enabled. Same Wi‑Fi as the TV.
